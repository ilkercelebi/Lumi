Welcome to Lumi. Your privacy is at the core of our relationship app. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Data We Collect

To provide a seamless couple experience, we collect specific types of data:

  • Account Information: Email address (via Apple Sign-In or other methods) and unique User/Couple IDs.
  • Partner Connectivity: We link two users via a unique invitation code to enable shared missions and timelines.
  • User-Generated Content: Memories (photos/text), mission completions, mood updates, and messages stored securely in our database (e.g., Firestore).
  • Usage Metadata: App performance, crash reports, and basic analytics to improve your experience.

2. Purpose of Collection

We use your data to:

  • Synchronize daily tasks and memories between you and your partner.
  • Provide personalized notifications for relationship milestones.
  • Manage in-app purchases and coin balances via Apple StoreKit.
  • Improve app stability and feature development.
